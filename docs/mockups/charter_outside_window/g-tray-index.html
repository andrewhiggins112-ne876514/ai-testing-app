Adopt · adapt · reject

Adopt

  • Hollow stroke and a soft glow, in the Insights sense: the chat stays visible and clickable.
  • VS Code’s 48px activity rail: icon-only, tooltips, dark fill, a foot cluster under a hairline.
  • qTest / Xray session verbs: state, still, note, end.
  • Game Bar materials: quiet, dark, rounded, no lesson panel.

Adapt

  • Snipping’s region stroke, kept up for the whole session.
  • ShareX’s “toolbar follows the region,” moved to the side so the south edge of the hole stays clear.
  • A secondary sidebar that grows outward (+240px) for Note and for the still crop.
  • Our cyan token, not the Insights highlight color.

Reject

  • Snipping’s top pill as the primary control, and its full-desktop dim.
  • A south text dock glued to the hole.
  • A fat hint window with nudge arrows and ±.
  • A second cyan Manual rectangle. Purple or cream cards.
