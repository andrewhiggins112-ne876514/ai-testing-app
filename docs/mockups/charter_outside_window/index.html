Option A — OS ring + thin edge-glued dock

A hollow Win32 ring sits on the fake IDE chat. One thin dock is glued to the bottom edge of that ring. Manual stays open and draws no second cyan rectangle. Assist and Mist are off. Drag the header to move the ring and the dock together. Drag edges and corners to resize. The hole is click-through.

One capture surface, and the controls stay on the hole. The dock can cover the bottom of the framed chat.

Option B — Hybrid dual chrome

The in-app cyan rect is still visible in Manual, and an OS ring sits on the foreign app. Still and End exist only on the OS dock. The Manual rect is labeled a read-only ghost: it does not move, resize, or capture. The frame shows the dual-chrome risk on purpose, and this path is still pickable.

Manual keeps a visible mark of the session. Two cyan shapes are on screen, and the ghost looks draggable.
